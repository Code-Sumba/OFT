01How Organic Trace works

A batch is one harvest of one product, for example 250 kg of basmati rice from one farm. Each batch gets a code such as OT-2026-7KQ3MZ. As the batch moves along the supply chain, each person who handles it records the step on the website. Every step is also written to the Polygon blockchain, where nobody can change it afterwards.

RoleWhat they do on the website
ShopperScans the QR code or types the batch code. No account needed.
FarmerRegisters harvest batches, uploads the organic certificate, and hands certified batches to a processor.
CertifierChecks each batch's certificate, then approves it (with an expiry date) or rejects it. Can renew or revoke later.
Processor / DistributorReceives batches, then hands them to the next stage.
RetailerReceives batches, prints QR labels, and marks batches sold.
AdministratorApproves new accounts, blocks accounts, reviews fraud alerts and watches activity.
Rules the system enforces
  • A batch must be certified before anyone can transfer it, and its certificate must not have expired.
  • Batches move forward one stage at a time. A farmer cannot send straight to a retailer, and nothing can go backwards.
  • Only the current holder can move a batch, and only to someone in the next role.
  • Once recorded, a step cannot be undone or edited.

02Creating an account and logging in

Shoppers don't need an account. Everyone else in the supply chain does.

  1. Click Join the chain at the top right of any page.
  2. Enter your full name, your organisation (optional), your role, your email and a password of at least 8 characters.
  3. Click Create account. You'll see "Registration received".
  4. Wait for an administrator to approve your account. Until then, logging in shows "Your account is awaiting admin approval".
  5. Once approved, click Log in and enter your email and password. You go straight to your own dashboard.

For security, a session lasts 2 hours. After that, log in again. Click Log out (top right) on shared computers. Administrator accounts can't be created from the sign-up page.

03For shoppers: checking a product

  1. Scan the QR code on the pack with your phone camera. It opens the product's page in the browser; no app is needed.
  2. No scanner? Go to the home page, type the code printed under the QR (for example OT-2026-7KQ3MZ) into the box, and click Verify.

If you see a warning

Read the reasons under the score. "Batch not found" means the code doesn't exist. Check that you typed it correctly, and be careful if the product claims to be organic.

04For farmers

Register a harvest batch

  1. Fill in Product, Quantity and Unit (kg, quintal, tonne, litre, dozen or piece), Origin (village or district) and Harvest date. The harvest date can't be in the future.
  2. Under Organic certificate, choose the certificate file: PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 5 MB.
  3. Click Register batch. The button shows "Uploading certificate to IPFS…" and then "Recording on blockchain…". This usually takes 5–30 seconds.
  4. A green notice confirms the registration, and the new batch appears under My batches with status Awaiting certification.

Hand a certified batch to a processor

  1. When the certifier has approved the batch, a Transfer → button appears next to it.
  2. Choose the receiving processor, type the hand-over location (for example "MIDC Ambad, Nashik"), and click Confirm transfer.
Tip

"Waiting for certifier" means you just need to wait. "Certificate expired: ask the certifier to renew" means the batch can't move until a certifier renews it. Click any batch code to open its full details.

05For certifiers

  1. Under Awaiting review, click Review next to a batch.
  2. Click Open farmer's certificate ↗ and check the document.
  3. Choose Approve as organic and set Certificate valid until (it must be a future date). If you want to replace the farmer's file with your own, choose a Certificate file. Click Approve.
  4. Or choose Reject, then Reject batch. A rejected batch can't be transferred, and it scores 40 (suspicious).

Changing a decision later

Batches you have reviewed are listed under Reviewed batches until they are sold. The button there says Renew (expired certificate), Revise (still valid) or Re-review (rejected). Use it to extend the expiry date, or reject a batch to revoke its certification, which immediately stops any further transfers. Every decision stays in the batch's certification history.

06For processors and distributors

Both dashboards work the same way. In my custody lists the batches you hold now; Passed on / sold lists the ones you have handed on.

  1. Click Transfer → next to a batch.
  2. Choose who receives it (a processor sends to a distributor, a distributor to a retailer), and type the hand-over location.
  3. Click Confirm transfer. The batch moves to Passed on / sold.

A greyed-out Transfer button means the certification was revoked or the certificate has expired. Hover over it to see which.

07For retailers

Print the QR label

  1. Click QR label next to a batch you hold.
  2. Click Print label to print it (the page prints only the label), or Download SVG to send it to a label printer or designer.
  3. Stick the label on the shelf or the packs. Scanning it opens the product page described in section 3.

Mark a batch as sold

  1. Click Mark sold, type the store location, and confirm. This is the final step in the journey.

08The batch details page

Clicking a batch code anywhere opens its details: the batch facts, the full journey with blockchain links, and the certification history. Public trace page ↗ opens what shoppers see. Retailers holding the batch, and administrators, also get Print QR label.

09For administrators

The admin panel has five tabs.

Overview

Counts of batches, the average trust score, open fraud alerts and accounts waiting for approval (click Review → to jump to them), plus a 14-day activity chart and batches by stage.

Users

Filter by Pending, Approved, Blocked or All. Click Approve to let someone log in, or Block to stop them; blocking takes effect immediately, even if they are logged in. A blocked account can be approved again later. Check that a person really belongs to the organisation they entered before approving.

Fraud alerts

A list of batches that broke a trust rule (see section 10). An alert closes automatically when the problem is fixed, for example when a certificate is renewed. Dismiss hides an alert after you have looked into it, but it reopens if the rule is still broken.

Batches

Every batch on the platform, with a search box for code or product.

Gas analysis

Compares the blockchain cost of this design with a design that stores everything on-chain. It is mainly useful for project reports.

10How the trust score works

Each batch starts at 100. Every time someone looks it up, the system checks it again, including comparing it with the blockchain, and subtracts points for each problem it finds:

RuleProblemPoints
R1Not certified (yet)−40
R2The website's record doesn't match the blockchain−30
R3The certificate has expired−25
R4More than 3 hand-overs within one hour−10 for each extra one, at most −20
R5Rejected by the certifier (instead of R1)−60
ScoreBand
80–100Verified
50–79Caution. For example, a newly registered batch scores 60 until it is certified.
0–49Suspicious
