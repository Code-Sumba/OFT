01About Organic Trace

What is Organic Trace?

A website that records organic produce from the farm to the shop shelf. Every step (registration, certification, each hand-over and the sale) is saved on the Polygon blockchain. A shopper scans the QR code on the pack and sees the whole journey, the organic certificate and a trust score.

What problem does it solve?

Organic labels are easy to fake, and the paperwork behind them is scattered across farms, certifiers and traders. Shoppers can't check a claim, and honest farmers lose out to fake "organic" goods. Organic Trace gives every batch one shared record that nobody can quietly change, and that anyone can check in seconds.

Who uses it?

Farmers, organic certifiers, processors, distributors and retailers each have an account and a dashboard. Shoppers use it without an account. An administrator approves accounts and watches for suspicious batches.

What is a "batch"?

One harvest of one product from one farmer, for example 250 kg of basmati rice harvested on 1 November. It gets a code like OT-2026-7KQ3MZ. The code avoids the characters 0, O, 1, I and L, so it can't be misread on a label.

Does anyone need to install an app?

No. Everything works in a normal web browser on a phone or a computer.

02For shoppers

How do I check a product?

Scan the QR code on the pack with your phone camera. Or open the home page, type the code printed under the QR, and press Verify. No account is needed.

What does "✓ Verified organic" mean?

All four of these are true: a certifier approved the batch, the certificate hasn't expired, the trust score is 80 or more, and the record matches the blockchain exactly.

What does "⚠ Check before buying" mean?

At least one check failed. The reasons are listed under the score, for example "Certificate expired" or "Rejected by certifier". It doesn't always mean fraud, but you should be careful.

What is the trust score?

A number from 0 to 100. Each batch starts at 100 and loses points for problems: not certified −40, record doesn't match the blockchain −30, certificate expired −25, too many hand-overs in one hour up to −20, rejected −60. 80+ is verified, 50–79 is caution, below 50 is suspicious.

The page says "Batch not found". Is the product fake?

First check the code: type it exactly as printed. If it still isn't found, the code was never registered, so treat any organic claim on that pack with suspicion.

Can I see the actual certificate?

Yes. Click View on IPFS ↗ in the "Organic certificate" box.

What are the long codes such as 0x0131d0…667df1?

They are blockchain transaction IDs. Clicking one opens the public block explorer, where anyone can see that the step really was recorded, and when.

03For farmers and certifiers

Do I need a crypto wallet or cryptocurrency?

No. You log in with an email and a password. The platform sends the blockchain transactions for you and pays the fees.

What do I need to register a batch?

Product name, quantity and unit, origin (village or district), harvest date, and the organic certificate as a PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP file of up to 5 MB.

I made a mistake in a batch. Can I edit it?

No. Records can't be changed; that is what makes them trustworthy. If the details are wrong, ask the certifier to reject the batch, and register a new batch with the correct details.

Why can't I transfer my batch?

A batch can only be transferred once it is certified and the certificate hasn't expired. It can only go to an approved processor. "Waiting for certifier" means the certifier hasn't reviewed it yet.

(Certifier) What should I check before approving?

Open the farmer's certificate. Check that it is genuine, that it covers this farmer and this product, and that it is still valid. Set the expiry date to the date on the certificate. You can upload your own copy to replace the farmer's file.

(Certifier) Can I change my decision?

Yes, at any time until the batch is sold. Use Renew, Revise or Re-review under "Reviewed batches". Rejecting a certified batch revokes it and stops any further transfers immediately. Every decision stays in the history.

What happens when a certificate expires?

The batch loses 25 trust points, shoppers see a warning, and nobody can transfer it until a certifier renews it with a new expiry date.

04For processors, distributors and retailers

How do I receive a batch?

You don't have to do anything. When the previous holder transfers it to you, it appears under In my custody on your dashboard.

Can I send a batch back, or skip a stage?

No. Batches move forward one stage at a time: farmer → processor → distributor → retailer. The website and the blockchain both block anything else.

I can't find the person I want to send to.

Only approved accounts in the next role are listed. Ask them to sign up, and ask the administrator to approve them.

(Retailer) How do I make QR labels?

On your dashboard, click QR label, then Print label or Download SVG. Only the retailer holding the batch, or an administrator, can print it.

(Retailer) When should I mark a batch as sold?

When the batch has been sold to consumers. Marking it sold is the final step, and it can't be undone. Shoppers can still scan the label afterwards.

05Accounts and administration

Why must an administrator approve my account?

So that every entry on the record comes from a known, checked person. Without this, anyone could sign up as a "certifier" and approve their own goods.

I forgot my password.

There is no "forgot password" screen yet. Contact the administrator, who can set a new one (Document 2, section 11).

Why was I logged out?

Sessions last 2 hours. You are also logged out at once if your account is blocked.

(Admin) What should I do with a fraud alert?

Open the batch and read the reason. Contact the people involved. For example, ask the certifier to renew an expired certificate, or investigate a "records do not match" alert. Alerts close by themselves when the problem is fixed. Dismiss only hides an alert, and it reopens if the problem remains.

(Admin) Can I delete a batch or an account?

Batches: no. Blockchain records are permanent. Accounts can be blocked, which stops them at once and can be undone by approving them again.

06Blockchain, security and privacy

Why use a blockchain at all?

A normal database can be edited by whoever runs it. A blockchain record is copied across thousands of independent computers and can't be rewritten. So even the platform owner can't secretly change a batch's history, and anyone can check it for themselves.

Which blockchain, and why?

Polygon, a widely used network that is compatible with Ethereum, where each transaction costs a fraction of a rupee. The pilot runs on Amoy, Polygon's test network, where fees are paid in free test tokens.

What if someone edits the website's database?

Every time a batch is looked up, the system compares the database with the blockchain. Any difference (product, owner, status, a hand-over) shows up at once as "Records do not match blockchain": the batch loses 30 points, shoppers see a warning, and the admin gets an alert. The blockchain also refuses any invalid move, such as skipping a stage, even if the database were wrong.

What personal data is public?

On the shopper page: participants' names, organisations, hand-over locations and dates. Email addresses and passwords are never shown. On the blockchain, people appear only as anonymous codes (a fingerprint of their account ID), never by name.

How are passwords and logins protected?

Passwords are stored only as secure bcrypt hashes. The login session is held in a cookie that page scripts can't read. Repeated failed logins are slowed down. Every request checks that the account is still approved.

Are certificate files public?

Yes. They are stored on IPFS, a public file network, so shoppers can see them. Don't upload documents that contain private information you wouldn't want shown.

Could someone copy a QR code onto a fake product?

A copied code still shows the original batch: its farmer, quantity and journey, including whether it was already sold. A shopper who sees "Sold", or a product that doesn't match, can tell something is wrong. Physical tamper-proof labels would make this stronger (see section 7).

07Costs, limits and the future

How much does it cost to run?

On the test network, blockchain fees are free. On Polygon mainnet, one batch's full journey uses about 1.14 million gas, roughly 0.034 POL at 30 gwei. Running costs are the server, a paid Supabase database, and optionally a paid Pinata plan.

Why not store everything on the blockchain?

It costs much more. Storing the certificate file and readable history on-chain used 2.46 million gas per batch, against 1.14 million for this design: 53.6% less. Only IDs, short fields and the certificate's fingerprint go on-chain; the file is on IPFS and the details are in the database.

What are the current limitations?
  • It runs on a test network; moving to Polygon mainnet is a planned step.
  • No "forgot password" screen.
  • The platform wallet signs on behalf of participants, so they trust the platform to sign what they asked for. The rules are still enforced by the contract, and all records are public.
  • One QR per batch, not per individual pack.
  • English only.
What could be added later?

Password reset by email, Hindi and Marathi screens, per-pack QR codes, tamper-evident labels, SMS updates for farmers, direct integration with certification bodies, IoT temperature and humidity logging during transport, and participants signing with their own wallets.

08Troubleshooting

ProblemWhat to do
"Your account is awaiting admin approval"Wait for the administrator, or contact them.
"Your account has been blocked"Contact the administrator.
"Invalid email or password"Check for typing mistakes and Caps Lock. After repeated failures, wait 15 minutes.
A green or amber notice says the transaction is pendingWait; don't submit again. It appears by itself, usually within a minute.
"Blockchain network is unavailable, please try again shortly"Try again in a few minutes. If it continues, the administrator should check the platform wallet's balance.
A batch shows "(confirming…)" for a long timeTell the administrator; the background indexer may have stopped.
File upload failsUse PDF, PNG, JPEG or WebP under 5 MB. "File uploads are not configured" is a server setting for the administrator.
The QR code opens the wrong website or "localhost"The server's public address is set wrongly. The administrator must fix NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL before printing more labels.
"Too many requests, please slow down"Wait a minute and reload.
The page looks broken or oldReload the page. If that doesn't help, clear the browser cache.
